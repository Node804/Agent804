# Technical debt

<!-- For a small project without a tracker. If there is a tracker, use it instead and keep

## DEBT-1: <the problem, in one line>

- Where: <files, components, who depends on it>
- Consequence: <what it actually costs: slower changes, repeated workarounds, failures>
- Owner: <a person or role>
- Decision: <repay now, schedule, or live with it, and who decided; an agent cannot decide>
- Look again: <date or event>
- Done when: <the outcome that shows it is gone>
- Closed: <revision and evidence, once done>

A debt item never waives a failing check, a security finding or a defect.
